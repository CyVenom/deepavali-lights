The five days.

  1. 1

    Dhanteras

    A day for health and prosperity. Many families buy something small and shiny.

  2. 2

    Naraka Chaturdashi

    Remembers the defeat of the demon Narakasura. Lamps are lit before dawn.

  3. 3

    Lakshmi Puja

    The main night. Every window glows, and prayers welcome Lakshmi in.

  4. 4

    Govardhan Puja

    Remembers Krishna lifting a hill to shelter his village. Food is offered and shared.

  5. 5

    Bhai Dooj

    Siblings honour each other with a tilak, a gift and sweets.

Why we light the lamp.

Rama comes home

After fourteen years in exile, Rama returned to Ayodhya. The city lined its streets with lamps to light his way.

Lakshmi visits

Lakshmi is said to enter the homes that are clean, open and bright. So the doors stay wide and the lamps stay lit.

Krishna wins

In much of the south, the night remembers Krishna ending the tyrant Narakasura, and a morning that finally arrived.

At home.

  • An oil bath at dawnWarm sesame oil, then prayers, before anyone eats.
  • A kolam at the doorRice flour and colour, drawn fresh to welcome guests.
  • New clothesSilk saris, kurtas and a lot of gold thread.
  • Open houseMurukku, laddu and a table that never really empties.
